Portfolio

Communication Beyond Words

My portfolio looks at how people express, communicate, and exchange love without having to speak. A love language is not a language in the literal sense; it is a way of naming the different forms love can take when one person communicates it to another.

Acrylic, charcoal, pencil, mixed media, and 3D work. Select any piece to see it larger.

The Five Love Languages

Five pieces, one for each love language, in paint, mixed media, and found objects.

01

Words of Affirmation

Mixed media: acrylic paint, colored pencil, and digital

This piece explores words of affirmation. The words appear as writing and art rather than speech. The wrinkled paper in the background is painted in acrylic, and the letter sits on top of it. The small figures climbing and sitting on the words show how alive words can be. A letter is only words on paper, but the meaning in it and the act of giving it bring the words to life and let the reader step into the world of the letter for a moment.

02

Physical Touch

Mixed media: acrylic paint and digital

This piece explores physical touch. The two hands cupping each other show love exchanged through physical contact. Inside the hands I placed words associated with love to represent the feeling that is carried by human touch.

03

Gift Giving

Acrylic

This piece explores gift giving. The present box overflowing with treasure and the bouquet beside it show love expressed through gifts. In the biggest jewel I painted a human face to represent the thought we give to the person receiving a gift. In gift giving, love is communicated not only through the object but through the care for the recipient that comes with it.

04

Quality Time

3D: clock, clay, paint, cardboard, plastic, and fabric

This piece explores quality time. The clock stands for time. Inside the hollowed-out clock, a family sits around a dinner table playing board games, eating, and talking, which shows the beauty of time spent with the people you love and how it builds connection.

05

Acts of Service

3D: acrylic paint, heart model, metal bowl, tin foil, tape, beads, glue, and flowers

This piece explores acts of service. The metal serving tray lid stands for the act of serving someone. Under the lid, the painted heart represents the love inside acts of service. The beads and flowers around the heart soften the look of the organ and present the heart, and love, as a treasure.
